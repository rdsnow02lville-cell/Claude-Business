Doximity and PracticeMatch help you find and message physicians. MatchedMD tells you what happens after you hire one.

FOR THE PRACTICE

Your own retention number

Every sizable group already has a 24-month new-hire retention rate. We've computed yours from public records and benchmarked it against your specialty — the baseline your next hire will beat or repeat. Most groups have never seen it.

FOR THE MATCH

Retention risk before you sign

Score a candidate on what actually predicts staying: geographic and family anchoring, career trajectory, training-network ties to your group, and your group's own track record — plus a confidential fit questionnaire that captures what no public record can.

FOR THE PIPELINE

Who to recruit next

We stage the national trainee pipeline by predicted completion date and rank the residents and fellows most likely to stay at your group specifically — by shared training origins, regional ties, and fit. Outbound targeting, not a job board.

Built by a physician who hires physicians.

MatchedMD was founded by a practicing orthopedic spine surgeon — a shareholder in a ~120-physician private group and the director of a surgical fellowship. The model was validated against two decades of his own group's hiring history before it was shown to anyone else. This exists because we've sat on both sides of the table and watched good hires fail for reasons that were visible all along.

The part public data can't see.

Federal records reveal geography, career history, and group track record. They can't see a spouse's career, a broken promise about call, or whether someone already feels like they belong. An eight-minute confidential instrument captures exactly that — and measurably sharpens the prediction.

Administered to consenting physicians, it turns the unobservable into eight validated fit indices — spousal anchoring, expectation-reality gaps, compensation clarity, belonging, and exit intent — each linked to real retention outcomes.
