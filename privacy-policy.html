Who we are

MatchedMD ("we," "us") operates matchedmd.ai, a store selling downloadable document templates for private medical, dental, and therapy practices.

Information we collect

  • Website usage. Our site is a static website. We do not require accounts, and we do not use advertising trackers on this site.

How we use information

  • To deliver your purchase and provide customer support.
  • To send transactional messages such as receipts (sent by Stripe).
  • To comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you have opted in.

Third-party services

We rely on service providers to run the store: Stripe (payments and receipts), GitHub Pages (website hosting), and social platforms where we publish content (such as Pinterest). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Data retention

Order records are retained as long as needed for support, tax, and accounting purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Children

Our products and website are intended for business use by adults and are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted at this address.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: reply to your Stripe receipt email, and it will reach us.
